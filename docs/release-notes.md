**Sruthi 0.4.2: share your month and your whole run.**

- **My month card.** A calendar of every day you dictated that month, shaded by how much, with your words, time skipped, best day and streak.
- **All time card.** Your whole run on one card: total words since your first day, the climb as a curve with your word milestones marked, and your speed, streak and dictation count.
- **About Sruthi.** Settings now shows the version and links to the source code, releases and third-party notices.
- **Checkable downloads.** Each release now includes a `SHA256SUMS.txt` and build provenance attestations, so you can check that an installer is the file GitHub built.
- **Source headers and notices.** Every source file carries its GPL license header, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` lists the licenses of what Sruthi builds on.

Everything from 0.4.1 is included: history across days with a day wheel and heat calendar, shareable stats cards, send after pasting, audio storage controls, snippets and the learning dictionary.

Windows is the primary platform. The macOS build is unsigned (right-click the app and choose Open on first launch) and the app-aware features need Windows. The Windows installer is not code-signed, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC"; choose More info, then Run anyway.
