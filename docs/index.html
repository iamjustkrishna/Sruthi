Why Sruthi

Faster than typing, and entirely yours.

Most of us speak two to three times faster than we type. Most dictation tools send your voice to someone else's server. Sruthi does the listening on your own machine.

Private by design

Recording, transcription and clean-up run on your PC. No account, no telemetry, nothing uploaded.

Works in every app

Browser, editor, chat, email, terminal. If it has a text box, Sruthi can type into it.

Yours to keep

Free and open source. Every dictation stays on your disk to replay, correct and search.

How it works

Three steps, then it is just there.

Install

Download the installer from the latest release and open it.

Pick a speech model

It downloads once. After that, dictation needs no internet at all.

Hold, speak, let go

Click into any text box and hold Ctrl Shift Space. Your words appear at the cursor.

Features

Everything around the dictation, too.

History

Every dictation, day by day.

Home keeps what you said, which app it went into and when. Step through days with a small wheel, pick a date from a heat calendar, or search all of it.

  • Replay a recording and fix a mishearing
  • Try another model on the same audio
  • See exactly what formatting changed
Stats

Your numbers, worth sharing.

See your speed against typing, your time saved and your streak. Turn them into a picture drawn on your PC, with only the parts you choose.

  • Time saved, My week, Speed and Milestone cards
  • Save a PNG or open X, LinkedIn or WhatsApp with a caption ready
  • No dictated text is ever on a card
Storage

Audio you control.

Keep audio for 90, 30 or 7 days, or not at all. Sruthi reminds you when it grows, and Free up space can zip it, move it to another drive or delete it.

  • Zips and copies are checked before anything is removed
  • Your words and stats always stay

Send after pasting

Sruthi presses the send key for you. Enter for chat, Ctrl+Enter for GitHub and mail, set per app or site.

Text that reads well

Numbers, dates, spoken punctuation and self-corrections are cleaned up, shaped to suit email, chat, documents or code.

Personal dictionary and snippets

Names spelled your way, and short phrases that type whole blocks of text.

Hinglish and more languages

English and 24 European languages, plus models that type mixed Hindi and English.

Runs on your graphics card

Larger models use your GPU when there is one, with an automatic fall back to the CPU.

A speed test on your voice

Correct a few dictations and Sruthi scores each model on what you actually said.

Privacy

You should not have to take our word for it.

What stays on your PC

  • Your voice. Recording and transcription run locally.
  • Your history. Recordings, words and stats live in a folder you can open, zip, move or delete.
  • Your keys. Any API key is kept in the system credential store.

The only times it uses the internet

  • Downloading a speech model, once, when you choose it.
  • Clean-up through a cloud provider, only if you add your own key, and then only the text.
  • The Share sheet's X, LinkedIn and WhatsApp buttons, which open your browser with just the caption. The picture stays on your PC until you paste it.
FAQ

Questions people ask.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. After a model is downloaded, dictation needs no internet at all.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC". Is that safe?

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows shows this for any new app from an independent developer. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The source is public if you would like to check it or build it yourself.

Where is my data?

In your user data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sruthi on Windows). Saved share cards go to Pictures\Sruthi and zipped audio to Documents\Sruthi.

Which model should I pick?

Start with Parakeet V3. Move to a Whisper model for whispers and rare words, or a Hinglish model for mixed Hindi and English.

Why does Windows show a microphone icon while Sruthi runs?

That is Instant start keeping the microphone ready so your first word is never missed. Audio is only kept while you hold the key, and you can turn Instant start off in Settings.

Is it as good as the cloud services?

It is close for everyday dictation and improves with your dictionary and corrections. The Models page has a speed test that scores each installed model on your own voice.

What about macOS?

There is an early, unsigned build. Dictation works, but features that need to know which app you are in are Windows-only for now.
