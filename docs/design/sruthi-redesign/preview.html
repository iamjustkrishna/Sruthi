What changed

Not a re-skin. The resource story is now part of every screen.

  • GPU and VRAM have exact numbers, engine split, and a 60-second chart.
  • CPU, RAM, heat pressure, and model runtime sit beside them.
  • Settings gained a live health strip, search, and categories.
  • Home, Dictionary, Snippets, and Models now show the work they do.
